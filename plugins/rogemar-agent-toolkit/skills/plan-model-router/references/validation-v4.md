for routine work, including implementation. Consequential judgment retains the

## Ranking and cost limits

Quality filtering uses the latest matching paired evidence and required executable
checks. Eligible candidates are ranked with their comparable historical role-level
Standard-credit proxy, then worker duration and raw tokens. Those historical observations
include differing context/cache and are not randomized causal cost rankings. Higher
effort alone cannot remedy a demonstrated defect. Max/Ultra remain available for exact
Default or future qualified exceptions, but lack new paired promotion proof here.

## Findings and limits retained in routing

- Routine lower-family coding is now possible where both the role and runnable coding
  exercise passed. The fresh resolver-to-worker probe applied Terra/xhigh to refactoring,
  verified native metadata and checked first-match identity and missing-ID behavior.
  Its Balanced parent was a policy fixture, not a live coordinator switch.
- Every tested Luna coding profile had at least one failure. Do not promote its prose
  result as executable implementation evidence. Economy work remains provisional with
  concrete checks and direct parent fallback; it cannot waive acceptance failures.
- Bug triage still omitted update or UI defects in multiple profiles. Keep the full
  issue inventory requirement and disclose parent omissions; higher effort alone was
  not a demonstrated cure.
- Source-grounding defects include saying request_id was absent when merely unused,
  treating proposed SQLite storage as existing, and claiming an assertion was executed.
  Qualifications remain focus-specific and cannot support fabricated runtime evidence.
- Hardest-tasks/hillclimb normally inherit the actual parent within mode limits. Exact
  qualified exceptions can cross families and reasoning in Lean/Economy only with a
  concrete need; passing at a higher profile does not establish that need.
- Native GoalBuddy Scout/Low and Judge/High remain independent read-only contracts.
  Their pin exemption applies only within the actual parent family, not arbitrary
  lower-family routing. Ordinary Forge assignments cannot replace a mandatory gate.

## Verification and usage accounting

The policy suite covers all225 mappings, actual-parent/mode ceilings, preferences,
aliases, consequential flags, executable evidence, exact Default, known failures,
qualified escalation and native pins. Independent review found and fixed two escapes:
an unqualified desired-target fallback and a broad native-role exemption. The new
regressions pass. Required repository/release checks and installation parity are
recorded in the local release receipt after the candidate is frozen.

The14 candidate/baseline workers used 1,510,775 raw tokens and 44.514 estimated Standard credits. Separately captured grading, policy-review and dispatch workers used 2,000,809 tokens and 111.574 estimated credits. These are evaluation overhead, not mode usage. Parent coordination tokens are not completely isolated; a complete execution cost is unavailable.
