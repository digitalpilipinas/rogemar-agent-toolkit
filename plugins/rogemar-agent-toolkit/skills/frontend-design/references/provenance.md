The reviewed `taste-skill-v1` and `gpt-tasteskill` variants share that stated
upstream and MIT notice. Their mandatory frameworks, fixed defaults, forced
animations and simulated execution instructions are not imported. This pack
adapts the current Taste brief-first/contextual approach and three design
controls into a focused optional reference. No private Taste preferences,
profile, user data, proprietary plugin cache or third-party assets were copied.

The frontend method retains one design direction, working UI, real states and
responsive craft. It replaces OD metadata injection with explicit contract
reading and host capability discovery, preserves existing product design and
uses the existing flow/token/interaction/motion owners instead of duplicating
their workflows. It grants no app/provider installation or publication authority.
