Choose the requested pass, not every pass:

- **Audit:** inspect hierarchy, type, spacing, color, states, responsiveness
  and accessibility; identify the highest-impact supported issues.
- **Critique:** explain how the current artifact serves or misses its brief.
  Distinguish usability defects from an optional aesthetic preference.
- **Polish:** make a few decisive scoped edits that preserve content, brand
  and the working flow. Remove redundant decoration before adding more.
- **Harden:** fix demonstrated overflow, text clipping, broken controls/links,
  contrast and missing interaction/failure states.
- **Present:** finish the requested artifact and its visual checks. Sharing,
  deployment or external publication is a separate authorized action.

Inspect before editing. Use actual rendered evidence when the issue depends
on layout or interaction, and state when that capability is unavailable. Do
not rebrand or restart the project unless requested. A repeated card layout,
bright accent or system font needs a product-specific reason to change it;
avoid turning anti-template guidance into arbitrary universal bans.

Check the changed moment across its applicable states: default, hover/focus,
active, loading, empty, error/retry, disabled and unavailable. Keep feedback
honest, preserve keyboard focus and reduced-motion behavior, and test the
affected narrow/short layout. Record observation, impact, proposed/implemented
change and verification in the shared finding record. Delegate motion only
through the existing coordinator when useful; this reference starts no agents.
