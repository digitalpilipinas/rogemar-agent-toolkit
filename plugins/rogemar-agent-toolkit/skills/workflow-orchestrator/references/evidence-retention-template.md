# Project evidence retention

Copy these fields into the project's existing verification or operations record.
Fill them from owner decisions; an unfilled template authorizes no deletion.
Keep project paths and durations out of the reusable toolkit contract.

- Policy owner and approval reference/date: `<owner; explicit approval>`
- Scope: `<exact artifact roots and classes; local/CI/archive locations>`
- Disposable intermediates: `<classes and eligibility age/event>`
- Final evidence: `<retention duration measured from verified acceptance/delivery>`
- Protected pins: `<artifact, reason, issue/audit reference, release owner>`
- Pin release: `<required resolution/acceptance facts and authorized releaser>`
- Cleanup operator and authority: `<who may run covered batches; approval scope>`
- Review date: `<when to review unresolved pins and storage growth>`
- Receipt: `<candidate paths, eligibility facts, pins excluded, deleted/retained results>`
- Human handoff: `<relevant artifact link or unavailable; finding ID; inspection and retention disposition>`

Before a covered batch, resolve paths inside the approved roots and reject symlinks
or traversal outside them. Exclude active writes, unresolved failures, required
final proof and audit/legal holds. Unknown ownership, missing delivery dates or
uncertain pin status mean retain and report. An age threshold never releases a pin.

Verify an accessible replacement and its contents before removing a needed local
copy. Preserve result bundles intact unless the owning tool documents safe pruning.
Replay caches, databases, user files and another session's resources are excluded
unless separately covered by explicit authority and their own lifecycle rules.

Preview and record the eligible set, recheck ownership/pins before deleting, and
report the outcome. Existing approval covers only its stated scope; obtain approval
for any expansion. Creating this policy does not schedule a cleanup job.
Human review alone does not release a pin. For deleted eligible media, retain the
finding and its resolution history, record the artifact as unavailable and link the
cleanup receipt. Report retained and failed items as well as deletions.
